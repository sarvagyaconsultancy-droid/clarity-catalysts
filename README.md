# MASTER WEBSITE PROMPT — SARVAGYA CONSULTANCY

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Create a modern, premium, highly visual and interactive website for:

# **Sarvagya Consultancy**

Sarvagya Consultancy is a finance, accounting and business advisory firm founded by **CMA Siddhanth Bothra**, with more than **10 years of professional experience in finance and accounts** and more than **4 years of operating Sarvagya Consultancy**.

Sarvagya is based in **Chennai, India**, with a team of 5 members. The team consists of qualified, semi-qualified and trained professionals personally trained by the founder.

The business primarily serves **Indian businesses**, while also being open to serving international clients.

Sarvagya combines:

**Corporate financial expertise + practical local business understanding**

to bring appropriate corporate structure, financial discipline and clarity to businesses without unnecessarily complicating things.

---

# 2. CORE WEBSITE OBJECTIVE

This website should NOT feel like a conventional accounting, CA or bookkeeping website.

Avoid the typical:

> “We provide quality accounting services, GST services and tax services.”

Instead, the website should make a business owner think:

> **“I didn't realize how badly my business needs this.”**

The website should create **problem recognition**, curiosity and urgency without using unethical fear tactics.

The visitor should recognize problems they may already be experiencing:

* Accounts are not properly managed
* Books are incomplete or inaccurate
* Business owners don't know what their accountant is actually doing
* Owners don't know what work should be derived from their in-house accountant
* Financial reports don't provide useful business insights
* Receivables/debtors are piling up
* GST/tax notices are difficult to handle
* Financial management is distracting the owner from sales and growth
* The owner spends too much time dealing with financial administration
* The business lacks proper financial structure

The website should then position Sarvagya as the solution.
